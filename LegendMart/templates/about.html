About LegendMart

LegendMart is a marketplace for buying and selling Mobile Legends: Bang Bang accounts.


How it works:

  • Sellers register with a valid ID and a payment method (GCash / Maya / PayPal).
  • Sellers upload account details (rank, skins, photos, price).
  • An admin reviews and approves each listing before it goes live.
  • Buyers pay directly to the seller — no middleman.
  • Both parties should keep proof of payment for safety.

Notice: Selling MLBB accounts violates Moonton's Terms of Service. Use this site responsibly. LegendMart is not affiliated with Moonton.
